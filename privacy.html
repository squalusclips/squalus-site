Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

Squalus Clips ("we", "us") publishes short-form gaming highlight clips on social media. Squalus Clips is operated from Cape Town, South Africa, by Wilhelm C. You can contact us at streamingclipsandmoments@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the internal Squalus Clips publishing tool (the "App"). The App is used only by Squalus Clips to upload and manage videos on social media accounts owned by Squalus Clips. It is not offered to the public, and it does not let other people sign in or connect their accounts.

3. Information the App accesses

When we connect our own accounts to the App, the App uses each platform's official API to access:

  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account name, profile picture, account ID).
  • Permission to upload and publish videos, captions and titles to our own accounts.
  • Statistics for our own videos (for example view, like and comment counts) so we can see how clips perform.

The App does not collect personal information from viewers of our content, does not access other users' private data, and does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

4. How we use and store this information

We use this information only to publish our own clips and measure their performance. Access tokens are stored securely on hardware we control and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, except to the platform itself when calling its API.

5. Platform services we use

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By using our content on YouTube, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The App also uses the TikTok and Instagram (Meta) APIs, which are governed by those platforms' own terms and privacy policies.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: via the Google security settings page.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram / Meta: in Accounts Center → Apps and websites.

Data deletion request: to ask us to delete any data the App holds that relates to you, email streamingclipsandmoments@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Stored API data is also deleted within 30 days after access is revoked.

7. Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us at the email above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
